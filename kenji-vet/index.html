About Us

A practice built on love
for all creatures.

Founded with a mission to eliminate the stress of traditional clinic visits, Kenji Vet Services sends our expert team directly to your location — farm, home, or stable — with everything needed for comprehensive veterinary care.



Our lead veterinary surgeon brings years of specialist experience treating everything from miniature hedgehogs to prize-winning bulls, with a personal touch that makes every animal feel safe.

What We Offer

Comprehensive
veterinary services.

From routine check-ups to complex surgeries, we bring the full clinic experience to your doorstep.

🏥

General Consultations

Thorough health assessments, vaccinations, and preventive care for pets and farm animals of all kinds.

🦜

Exotic Animal Care

Specialised care for birds, reptiles, small mammals and rare exotic species requiring specialist handling.

🐄

Farm & Livestock

Ambulatory large-animal medicine — cattle, goats, sheep, pigs — including herd health programmes.

🔬

Diagnostic Services

On-site blood work, urinalysis, and portable ultrasound for rapid, accurate diagnosis in the field.

Surgery & Procedures

Minor surgical procedures, castrations, wound management, and dental care performed on location.

💉

Vaccination Drives

Scheduled group vaccination campaigns for farms and communities at competitive package rates.
