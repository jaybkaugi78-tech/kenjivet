What We Offer

Everything your animal needs.

🏥

General Consultations

Thorough health assessments, vaccinations, and preventive care for pets and farm animals of all kinds.

🦜

Exotic Animal Care

Specialised care for birds, reptiles, small mammals and rare exotic species requiring specialist handling.

🐄

Farm & Livestock

Ambulatory large-animal medicine — cattle, goats, sheep, pigs — including herd health programmes.

🔬

Diagnostic Services

On-site blood work, urinalysis, and portable ultrasound for rapid, accurate diagnosis in the field.

⚕️

Surgery & Procedures

Minor surgical procedures, castrations, wound management, and dental care performed on location.

💉

Vaccination Drives

Scheduled group vaccination campaigns for farms and communities at competitive package rates.

Parasite Control

Comprehensive deworming, tick and flea management programmes tailored to your animal's needs.

🍽️

Nutrition Advice

Personalised dietary guidance for optimal health, performance and longevity of your animals.

🆘

Emergency Visits

24/7 emergency callouts — because animal health crises don't wait for business hours.

Care Specialties

Specialist care by category.

Companion Animals

🐕 Dogs & Cats

Full-spectrum care — vaccinations, neutering, dental health, nutrition, emergency treatment and geriatric care for your most beloved companions.

Exotic Pets

🦜 Birds & Reptiles

Specialist handling of parrots, finches, geckos, tortoises and other exotic species that require specific expertise and a calm environment.

Small Mammals

🐇 Rabbits & Rodents

Dental checks, gut stasis management, spaying, and general health assessments for rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters and hedgehogs.

Livestock

🐄 Cattle & Goats

Herd health programmes, reproduction management, tick treatment, foot care, and emergency obstetric services delivered on-farm.

Poultry

🐓 Chickens & Ducks

Flock vaccinations, Newcastle disease management, egg-laying health optimisation and biosecurity consulting for commercial and backyard flocks.

Aquatic

🐠 Fish

Water quality assessment, disease diagnosis and treatment for ornamental pond fish and aquarium species.

Don't see your animal?

We treat many more species than listed — call us to discuss your animal's specific needs.
